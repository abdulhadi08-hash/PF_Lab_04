1. An airline upgrades passengers to Business Class automatically if they are frequent flyer members (input
as 1 for yes, 0 for no) AND have flown more than 50,000 miles this year, OR if they paid more than Rs.
80,000 for their ticket regardless of membership. Take membership status, miles flown, and ticket price
as input, and decide if the passenger gets upgraded.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. A student wants to register for an "Advanced Programming" course, which requires that they have passed
"Programming Fundamentals" (input as 1 for passed, 0 for not passed) with a grade point of 2.5 or higher,
AND have completed at least 30 total credit hours. Take pass status, grade point, and completed credit
hours as input, and determine if the student can register for the course.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. An online store offers free delivery if the order amount is above Rs. 3000 OR if the customer is a premium
member (input as 1 or 0). Cash on Delivery (COD) is only available for orders below Rs. 50,000 and within
city limits (input as 1 for within city, 0 for outside). Take order amount, membership status, and city
status as input, and display the delivery charge status and COD availability.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. A factory machine automatically shuts down if its temperature exceeds 100°C OR its pressure exceeds
250 PSI. It enters "Warning Mode" if temperature is between 85-100°C AND pressure is between 200-250
PSI simultaneously. Take temperature and pressure readings as input, and determine the machine's
operating status.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. A telecom company offers four plans selected by number: Plan 1 (Rs. 500 for 1000 minutes), Plan 2 (Rs.
800 for 2000 minutes), Plan 3 (Rs. 1200 for unlimited minutes), Plan 4 (custom plan billed at Rs.
1/minute). Take the plan choice and minutes used (only needed for Plan 4) as input, and use switch to
calculate the total bill, checking if usage exceeds plan limits for Plans 1 and 2 to apply an extra per-minute
charge of Rs. 2.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. A university awards scholarships based on combined criteria: a "Full Scholarship" requires CGPA above
3.7 AND family income below Rs. 50,000/month; a "Half Scholarship" requires CGPA above 3.3 AND
family income below Rs. 100,000/month; otherwise, no scholarship is awarded. Take CGPA and monthly
family income as input, and determine the scholarship category.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. An elevator has a maximum weight capacity of 1000 kg and can carry a maximum of 10 people. Given the
number of people and total combined weight as input, determine if the elevator can operate normally,
needs to deny entry due to overweight, or needs to deny entry due to exceeding the people limit —
checking both conditions together.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. A traffic police system fines drivers based on a zone type entered as a number (1 = School Zone, 2 =
Highway, 3 = Residential Area) where each zone has a different speed limit (School = 30 km/h, Highway =
100 km/h, Residential = 50 km/h). If the driver's speed exceeds the zone's limit by more than 20 km/h,
the fine is doubled. Take zone type and driver's speed as input, and use switch combined with if-else to
calculate the final fine amount, given a base fine of Rs. 1000 for any violation.
